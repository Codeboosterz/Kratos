# 01 — Product brief

## Business outcome

Turn Kratos Fitness from a broad brochure-and-tools site into a focused Dutch conversion system that:

- explains Omar’s approach;
- routes visitors to the right training offer;
- converts uncertain visitors through an intake;
- preserves existing Trainerize program destinations;
- supports future internal Stripe checkout;
- keeps free tools available without allowing them to dominate the commercial funnel.

## Primary users

1. Local personal-training prospect who wants to lose weight, build muscle, or become fitter.
2. Remote prospect seeking online coaching.
3. Couple or training partner seeking duo coaching.
4. Self-guided buyer seeking a home-workout or digital program.
5. Returning buyer completing checkout or intake.

## Core journeys

### Guided journey

Home → goal/trajectory → product detail → intake → coach follow-up.

### Direct purchase journey

Home or campaign → product detail → verified external Trainerize page now, or internal Stripe checkout later → verified success → intake.

### Trust journey

Home → results → Omar → intake.

### Free-tool journey

Home/footer → free tools → local calculation → intake CTA.

## Success criteria

- One dominant CTA per viewport.
- All required products discoverable by category.
- All supplied plan URLs preserved centrally.
- No unknown price shown as fact.
- No fake Stripe success state.
- Mobile checkout and homepage optimized to 320 px and above.
- Every visible control has a route or typed action.
- Identity, claims, prices, and testimonials have explicit verification status.

## Required pages

Home, trajectories catalogue, dynamic product detail, results, Omar, free tools, intake, checkout, checkout success, privacy, terms, cookies, contact, and a legacy services redirect.

## Exclusions for this package

- No production codebase.
- No live payment processing.
- No Trainerize API automation.
- No database or CRM connection.
- No deployment.
- No invented plan mapping, price, certification, or outcome.
