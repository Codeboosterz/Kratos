# About Omar — `/over-omar`

## Job of the page

Build trust in the coach and method, then hand the visitor to an intake without turning the page into an unverifiable résumé.

## Section wiring

| Order | Component | Content source | Main action/state |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `AboutHero` | canonical approved portrait and short bio | intake CTA; image fallback |
| 2 | `CoachStory` | client-approved biography | no invented chronology |
| 3 | `Principles` | approved coaching principles | semantic cards/list |
| 4 | `Method` | verified coaching process | link to trajectories |
| 5 | `Credentials` | evidence-backed qualifications only | omit unresolved credentials |
| 6 | `PersonalCTA` | approved voice and contact promise | `/intake?source=about` |

## Identity and content contract

- The supplied campaign images show more than one apparent coach identity. The client must approve a canonical Omar reference set before any final portrait generation or selection.
- Do not regenerate or face-swap a real person for production without an approved identity source and permission.
- Do not publish “4 jaar ervaring,” client totals, qualifications, affiliations, or specialty claims until approved evidence is recorded.
- Retain realistic skin texture and athletic proportions; avoid extreme bodybuilding or superhero treatment.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop hero uses a balanced copy/portrait split. Mobile places the trust-building copy and CTA before nonessential biography detail.
- Long biography copy uses readable line length, not condensed display type.
- The primary CTA remains a semantic link/button and is not embedded in portrait artwork.
